 # MERIDIAN — Objects of time

Original editorial watch boutique concept. Real-time 3D mechanical film: a brushed-metal timepiece inside a monumental circular observatory. Five scroll chapters pass through an exploded movement and converge into a complete watch.

## Identity
- Near-black #171816, warm ivory #eeeae1, silver #a5a59b, vermilion #c55a36. Dark cinematic opening, warm paper collection, dark craft exhibit.
- Display: Cormorant Garamond, light and italic. UI: Manrope; widely tracked small uppercase labels.
- 64px desktop gutters, 24px mobile, thin rules, asymmetry, huge serif headlines, squared controls.
- Quiet wordmark, collection/craft/about links, outlined inquiry action, mobile menu.

## Motion
Three.js PBR materials, procedurally lit reflection environment, soft shadows, fog, atmospheric light shafts, particles, intricate geometry. GSAP ScrollTrigger drives five camera/assembly chapters in a sticky stage. Visual continuity through dolly, sweep, disassembly, reassembly. Global pause freezes visuals but keeps content accessible; reduced motion bypasses extended scroll.
Collection filtering and model detail dialogs. Monumental SVG escapement with rotating gears, drawn paths, selectable craft explanations. Scroll-driven horizontal stories with masked photography and parallax.

## Content
Fictional Meridian models, indicative USD prices, clearly labeled concepts. No real inventory or checkout. Inquiry creates a local downloadable request summary, never claims to send it. No API/database changes.

## Quality
Responsive, semantic landmarks, keyboard/dialog access, Escape to close, reduced motion, global pause, WebGL fallback. Cap pixel ratio, reuse resources, suspend offscreen rendering, GPU transforms. Verify build plus desktop/mobile browser flows.
